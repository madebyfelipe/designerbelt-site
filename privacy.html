Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Designer's Belt is built to work on your PC. This page explains what data we handle and why.

What stays on your device

Local tools, including font recognition, process your files and screen selections on your PC. We do not receive them.

What we collect

When the license goes on sale, Paddle will process purchase data such as name, email, country and payment status. When license activation is available, we will store the license key, a device identifier and label, and the activation date, so we can enforce the three-device limit.

Cloud

Cloud is optional. Files you choose to upload are stored in the bucket or service you configure. Details about Cloud accounts and billing will be added before Cloud is on sale.

Website

This website does not use advertising trackers. Our host may keep basic server logs for security.

Sharing

We share data only with the services we use to take payments and deliver your license, such as Paddle. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data at any time. Under LGPD and GDPR you also have the right to object and to data portability.
